# Paper Craft Diorama

Build a tabletop world from cut card, folded paper and printed illustrations. Choose a familiar physical object to carry the idea from the opening close-up into the wider model. For example, follow a parcel from a paper hand to a sorting depot, then pull back to show its route through a miniature neighborhood.

**Palette and materials.** Start with warm kraft or off-white stock. Use faded sky blue, sage and terracotta for scenery, with brick red for a route or point of emphasis. Give edges a little thickness. Use a fixed, subtle fiber texture so it stays attached to the sheet as it moves. Vary cut contours and show folds where a prop bends.

**Camera and light.** Cut between overhead tabletop arrangements and low oblique views into a miniature street or building. Separate foreground cutouts, upright props and the illustrated backdrop so a slow camera move reveals depth. Use a broad upper-side light with soft directional shadows. Keep a short contact shadow beneath every raised piece.

**Motion.** Let a paper hand place the opening object. Unfold a panel, slide a card sideways to reveal the next example, or trace a narrow paper ribbon across a map. Carry an object or accent color through the cut. Hold the assembled scene long enough to read it.

**Typography.** Print short labels directly on model signs. Set narration captions in dark sans type on a small rounded off-white paper slip near the lower center. Keep the slip in screen space and clear of the main action.

**Implementation.** For flat shots, stack textured SVG silhouettes at shallow depth with offset soft shadows. For camera moves through a model, use thin extruded Three.js shapes and matte materials. Create original illustrations for the topic. Preview a close-up and a wide shot to check visible paper edges, grounded shadows, and legible captions.
