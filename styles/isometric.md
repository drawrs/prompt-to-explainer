# Isometric
Palette: warm ivory #f4f0e7, charcoal ink #243036, teal #23877a for active paths, amber #d98732 for changes.
Camera: orthographic at equal x/y/z offsets looking at the origin, with vertical world edges upright. For SVG project ground axes at ±30 degrees. Use consistent tile dimensions across chapters.
Lighting: broad upper-left key, three distinct face values, contact shadows falling right. Give the hero the strongest silhouette.
Typography: screen-space sans labels and short horizontal captions. Use leader lines when a label would cover a neighboring tile.
Motion: assemble modules in depth order, trace a path from input to output, then hold the completed diagram. Scale the orthographic view for close-ups. Keep projection angles stable across cuts.
